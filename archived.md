# Archived 8bf Filters

These creators' sites are gone, and the only copies left are in the Internet Archive. All of these filters are 32-bit.

**Don't visit the old live domains.** xero-graphics.co.uk, 8bf.net and littleinkpot.co.uk now belong to spammers.

- [Xero Classic](https://web.archive.org/web/2016/http://www.xero-graphics.co.uk/) - 40 subtle artistic and photo effects, including Porcelain, Lineart and Soft Vignette. By Pete Bailey, Xero Graphics.
- [AAA Filters and Frames](https://web.archive.org/web/2012/http://8bf.net/) - AAA Options, Blender, Foto Frame, Frame Works and Multi-Filter. By Jim Clatfelter.
- [Little Ink Pot](https://web.archive.org/web/2006/http://www.littleinkpot.co.uk/) - Xpose, Thredgeholder and Chalkaholic Lite. By Little Ink Pot, later Software Geezers.
- [Mura's Meister](https://www.oocities.org/murakuma/) - Copies, Cloud, Perspective Tiling and Pole Transform, in LHa archives. By MuRa. GeoCities mirror.
- [Redfield Classic Freeware](https://web.archive.org/web/2016/https://www.redfieldplugins.com/) - Jama 3D, Lattice Composer, Seamless Workshop, Water Ripples and Craquelure 3D. By Redfield Plugins.
