# Contributing

Open a pull request or an issue.

A filter belongs here if:

- it's an `.8bf` filter, or a tool for making them;
- it's free to download and use (freeware, open source, donationware, or a
  free version with honest limits stated in the row);
- it can still be downloaded, from the creator or the Internet Archive.

For each addition, give the creator's own page (never a download mirror or a
re-upload), whether it's 64-bit, its license, and one line on what it does.
Keep the table sorted with the most useful filters first.

Never link a domain that has lapsed and been taken over; link an Internet
Archive snapshot instead.

Compatibility reports ("loads in Affinity 2 on Windows", "crashes in
8bf-host under Wine") are welcome as issues.
